👋 How to get your Amazon data
▼
myshoplight turns your Amazon order history into a clear picture of exactly where your money goes — by category, by year, by audience, and more. To get started, you'll need to download your order data directly from Amazon. Here's how:
1
Log into your Amazon account, then click this link and submit your data request.
2
Amazon will email you immediately — open it and click the "Confirm Data Request" button. Once confirmed, they'll send a second email with your download link typically within 24–48 hours.
3
Download the Your Orders.zip file and open it. The two files you'll need are "Order History.csv" (in the Your Amazon Orders folder) and optionally "Refund Details.csv" (in the Your Returns & Refunds folder, only needed if you want returned items excluded). Upload them below. ⬇️

Returns Manager
Returned items are excluded from your dashboard so your numbers reflect what you actually spent. Review and resolve any items below where we couldn't auto-match a refund.
How does return matching work?
✓ Auto-matched means we're confident the refund matches a specific item — either the order had only one item, or the refund amount uniquely matches exactly one item in a multi-item order. No action needed — these are already excluded from your dashboard.
⚠ Needs Review means the order had multiple items and the refund amount doesn't uniquely identify which one was returned (e.g., two items at the same price, or a partial refund that doesn't match any single item). You'll pick which item was returned and whether it was a full or partial return.
Partial returns happen when you bought multiple of an item (qty > 1) and only returned some. Use the "Partial return" button — only the refund amount is subtracted from the item's price, and the rest still counts as spending.
Unmatched Refunds are refunds Amazon recorded for orders that aren't in your uploaded Order History — usually purchases from years you didn't include or filtered-out addresses. These are shown for transparency but don't require action.
