What happens next

2

Request your Amazon order history

Head to Amazon's privacy portal and request your order data. Select "Your Orders" and click Submit Request.

Go to Amazon's data request page →

Takes ~24 hours
3
Don't skip this

Confirm your request via email from Amazon

Amazon sends a confirmation email right after you submit. You must click the button inside to start processing — without it, your data file will never be sent. Look for the subject line:

✉ Your Data Request Confirmation
Easy to miss
4

Upload when your data file arrives

Once Amazon processes your confirmed request, you'll receive an email with your file. Look for the subject line:

✉ Your Data Request

Log in to myshoplight from a desktop browser, upload the file, and your entire Amazon history is ready to explore.
