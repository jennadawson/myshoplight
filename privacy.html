Privacy policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Last updated: July 21, 2026

myshoplight ("myshoplight," "we," "us," or "our") is a product of Caliet Ventures LLC. This policy explains what information we collect when you use myshoplight, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. myshoplight is built around a simple idea — helping you understand your own Amazon spending — so keeping your data private and under your control matters to us.

myshoplight is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Amazon.com, Inc. We only ever see the order-history data that you choose to request from Amazon and upload to us. We never have access to your Amazon account, your login, or your payment methods at Amazon.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are handled through our authentication provider and stored in hashed form — we never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Your Amazon order data. myshoplight works by analyzing the order-history report that you request directly from Amazon and upload to the app. This may include item descriptions, order dates, quantities, and amounts. This data is used to build your dashboard and stays associated with your account.
  • Your customizations. Categorization rules you add, category overrides, custom categories, and similar settings you create in the app.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback through the app, we collect your message and, if you choose to include it, your email address so we can reply.

Information collected automatically

Like most web applications, our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically process basic technical information — such as your IP address, browser type, and request logs — as needed to deliver the service, keep it secure, and diagnose problems. myshoplight does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Payment information

Purchases are processed by our payment provider, Stripe. When you buy myshoplight, your card details are entered directly with Stripe and are handled under Stripe's own security and privacy practices. We do not receive or store your full card number. We receive confirmation of your purchase and limited transaction details needed to grant you access.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service — categorizing and visualizing the Amazon order data you upload.
  • To create and manage your account and verify your access to the product.
  • To process your purchase and deliver your license.
  • To send you essential account emails, such as your welcome email and password-related messages.
  • To respond to your feedback and support requests.
  • To maintain, secure, debug, and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

How your data is stored

Your account information, uploaded order data, and customizations are stored in our database, hosted by Supabase. Access is protected by row-level security so that your data is associated with your account and is not readable by other users of the app. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share information only with the service providers that make myshoplight work, and only as needed for them to perform their function:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of your account data.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Resend — sending transactional emails (welcome and password emails).
  • Netlify — application hosting and delivery.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition. In that event we will continue to protect your information under the terms of this policy.

Data retention

We keep your account information and uploaded data for as long as your account is active so that your dashboard remains available to you. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain limited records (for example, transaction records) to meet legal or accounting obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing us at jenna@myshoplight.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to request a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. We will honor these rights as required by applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Children's privacy

myshoplight is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

International users

myshoplight is operated from the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States. If you access the service from outside the U.S., you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S., where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Your continued use of myshoplight after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, reach out any time at jenna@myshoplight.com.
