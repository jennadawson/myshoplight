Questions

A few things people ask before buying

How do I get my Amazon order history?

You request it directly from Amazon through their data privacy portal. It takes about 24 hours to arrive in your inbox as a downloadable file. After you purchase myshoplight, we send you a step-by-step guide so you know exactly what to request and where to find it.

Is my data safe? Does myshoplight store my order history?

Your Amazon data is stored securely in your myshoplight account — it's never sold or shared with anyone. We don't connect to Amazon directly and we never ask for your Amazon password.

How far back does it go?

As far back as Amazon's data export covers — typically several years of order history. The more history you have, the more patterns myshoplight can surface.

We share an Amazon account. Will it still work?

Yes. myshoplight filters by shipping address, so even a shared account breaks down cleanly by household. And if you and your partner have separate accounts, you can upload both and see everything together in one dashboard.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — myshoplight works across all your devices and your dashboard is accessible on phone, tablet, or desktop once your data is uploaded. That said, we recommend doing the initial file upload from a computer. Downloading your Amazon data export and uploading it is a bit easier to manage on a desktop browser. After that, checking in on your spending from your phone works just fine.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase of $27. You get access to myshoplight on any device, forever — no monthly fees, no renewal reminders.

What if the categories don't match how my family shops?

myshoplight starts with 2,000+ built-in categorization rules, but every family is different. You can customize any category to match your household's actual habits — so your dashboard reflects your life, not a generic template.
