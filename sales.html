Questions

A few things people ask before buying

Couldn't I just put my order history into ChatGPT?

You can, and for a quick one-off question it will do a decent job. The difference is that this is a dashboard, not an answer. Your file comes back sorted the same way every time, the categories stay put so this year is comparable to last year, you can correct a category once and have it stick, and it is all still there next month without re-uploading or re-explaining. A chat gives you one reading of your data. This gives you somewhere to keep it.

How do I get my Amazon order history?

You request it directly from Amazon through their data privacy portal. It takes about 24 hours to arrive in your inbox as a downloadable file. The full walkthrough is free and right here — you do not have to buy anything to follow it.

Is my data safe? Does myshoplight store my order history?

Your Amazon data is stored securely in your myshoplight account — it's never sold or shared with anyone. We don't connect to Amazon directly and we never ask for your Amazon password.

How far back does it go?

As far back as Amazon's data export covers — typically several years of order history. The more history you have, the more patterns myshoplight can surface.

Our household doesn't map neatly to one Amazon account. Does that break it?

No — that is the normal case, not the edge case. One account covering several households (still on your parents' Prime, say) sorts by shipping address, so you see only what came to your door. One household spread across several accounts works the other way: upload them all and they land in one dashboard together. Either shape, you end up with your household's real picture rather than whatever the login happens to contain.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — the full myshoplight experience works on any device. Upload your file, explore your dashboard, and check back on your spending from your phone, tablet, or desktop. Nothing requires a computer.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase of $27. You get access to myshoplight on any device, forever — no monthly fees, no renewal reminders.

What if the categories don't match how my family shops?

myshoplight starts with 3,000+ built-in categorization rules, but every family is different. You can customize any category to match your household's actual habits — so your dashboard reflects your life, not a generic template.
